# Flags — Portuguese (prt)

## MOTTO
- `religion.preamble.headlineGrad` / `religion.preamble.headlineLine` / `religion.book.trust` / `legal.motto` / `about.footer.motto`: Translated "In God We Trust" → "Em Deus confiamos" (the well-known Portuguese rendering of the US motto) → God→Dog swap yields "Em Cão Confiamos". The parody works because "Cão" (dog) vs "Deus" (God) are clearly distinct and the original phrase is recognizable to Portuguese speakers. However, the motto is less culturally embedded in Portugal/Brazil than in the US, so the parody impact may be softer for some audiences. Considered "Em Deus Confiamos" vs "Em Deus nós confiamos" — went with the shorter 3-word form as a direct parallel. Human review recommended for `about.footer.motto` casing ("Em cão confiamos." lowercase, matching EN "In dog we trust.").
