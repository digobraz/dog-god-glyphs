# Translation flags — Ukrainian (ukr)

## Motto: "In Dog We Trust"
- **Keys:** `religion.preamble.headlineGrad` / `religion.preamble.headlineLine` / `religion.book.trust` / `legal.motto` / `about.footer.motto`
- Ukrainian has no single canonical phrase "In God We Trust" the way English does (it's the US national motto, not widely known in Ukraine as a fixed phrase). The closest religious phrase in Ukrainian is "На Бога уповаємо" (We trust in God / We rely on God), which is recognizable to educated speakers through the Bible (Psalm 56:11 etc.) and is the direct structural parallel.
- Swap: Бога → Пса → **"На Пса уповаємо"** / split: "На Пса" + "Уповаємо".
- Parody recognizability: moderate — readers familiar with the US motto (via film/culture) will catch it; purely Ukrainian-context readers may not immediately see the "In God We Trust" reference but will understand "We Trust in the Dog" as a joke.
- Alternative considered: "Псу ми довіряємо" (more colloquial) — rejected as less ceremonial/parody-effective.
- **Recommendation:** use "На Пса Уповаємо" but consider a tooltip or subtitle referencing the US motto for readers unfamiliar with it.
