# Translation flags — Italian (ita) — 2026-06-17

## MOTTO: "In Dog We Trust"

**Keys:** `religion.preamble.headlineGrad` / `religion.preamble.headlineLine` / `religion.book.trust` / `legal.motto` / `about.footer.motto`

**Issue:** Italian does not have a widely-known native equivalent of "In God We Trust" the way English-speaking cultures do. The US motto is occasionally cited in Italian as "In Dio noi confidiamo" or "In Dio ci fidiamo", but these phrases are not culturally embedded — a typical Italian speaker may not immediately recognise the parody source.

**Rendered as:** "In Cane Noi Confidiamo" (swap Dio→Cane). The parody is grammatically sound and a bilingual/culturally aware Italian will catch it, but the pun's resonance is weaker than in English.

**Alternatives considered:**
- "In Dio confidiamo" → "In Cane confidiamo" (shorter, more natural Italian)
- Keep EN "In Dog We Trust" entirely (loses localisation)
- "In Cane ci fidiamo" (colloquial; loses the solemn register of a motto)

**Recommendation:** "In Cane Noi Confidiamo" is the strongest rendering for the solemn/parodic register. Human review recommended before deploy — the split headlineGrad/headlineLine ("In Cane" / "Noi Confidiamo") reads naturally as a two-line headline.
