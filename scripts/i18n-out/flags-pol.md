# Flags — Polish (pol) translation

## MOTTO — "IN DOG WE TRUST"

**Parody target:** Polish has no exact constitutional/institutional motto equivalent of "In God We Trust", but the phrase is widely known via American culture and often rendered as "W Bogu ufamy" in Polish media.

**Rendering chosen:** "W Bogu ufamy" → swap Bogu (God, dative) → Psie (dog, dative) → **"W Psie ufamy"**

**Why it works:** A Polish speaker recognises the American motto parody immediately via the dative construction "W [X] ufamy". The swap Bogu→Psie is clean and grammatically natural.

**Split keys:**
- `religion.preamble.headlineGrad` = "W Psie"
- `religion.preamble.headlineLine` = "Ufamy"
- `religion.book.trust` = "W Psie Ufamy"
- `legal.motto` = "DOGYPT · W Psie Ufamy"
- `about.footer.motto` = "W psie ufamy." (sentence-case per source)

**Confidence: ~90%** — flagged because "Psie" (locative of pies) is grammatically correct but the resulting phrase sounds slightly unusual to a Polish ear that expects "Bogu". Recommend native speaker review before deploy.
